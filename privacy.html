Privacy

Last updated: 5 August 2026.

Website data

This static website does not operate an account database and does not store registration form entries on its server.

Founding-player form

The form prepares an email in the visitor's own email application. The visitor decides whether to send it. Details are handled by the visitor's email provider and the receiving mailbox.

Wallet features

The Add PLVIP function asks a compatible wallet to display the official token. The website does not receive recovery phrases or private keys and will never ask for them.

Third-party links

BaseScan and wallet software are operated by third parties with their own privacy terms.
