THE STORY

Achievements should follow the player.

Every game creates moments worth remembering: a first victory, a difficult rank, a team built from scratch or a tournament run nobody expected. Yet those achievements are scattered across platforms.

Players League VIP is being built as a shared layer for gaming identity, recognition and community competition: one profile, a growing record of achievements and a league where reputation is earned through participation.

One global league. Every player has a story. Every achievement should count.

FAQ

Clear answers.

Do I need PLVIP to create a player profile?

No. Player profiles, public identity and community challenges do not require a wallet or token purchase.

Do leaderboard points have financial value?

No. Community points are non-transferable, cannot be purchased and have no monetary value.

Can PLVIP be bought today?

The token exists on Base, but this website does not currently announce an active official liquidity pool or public trading launch.

Is PLVIP an investment?

No promise of profit, value growth or resale is made. PLVIP may lose some or all value and future utility is not guaranteed.
