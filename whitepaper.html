01

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Players League VIP begins with identity, not speculation.

Players build skill, friendships, reputations and memories across games, communities and seasons. Yet much of that history becomes fragmented or invisible when platforms change. Players League VIP is a developing concept for a global gaming identity and recognition layer designed to help player progress become part of one growing story.

PLAY

Participation first

The starting point is the player and their activity—not a required token purchase.

COMPETE

Recognition earned

Future challenges should value skill, consistency, teamwork and fair play.

RISE

Progress remembered

The long-term ambition is to help achievements and reputation remain meaningful over time.

02

THE PROBLEM

Gaming identity is fragmented.

Progress is trapped

Achievements and status often remain locked inside one title, one season or one platform.

Recognition is incomplete

Traditional rankings may capture scores but miss teamwork, leadership, consistency and community contribution.

Communities are disconnected

Players often rebuild profiles, reputations and social connections whenever they move.

Ownership language is confusing

Token projects can overpromise. Players need clear facts, visible risks and honest product status.

03

THE VISION

One global league. Many ways to matter.

Players League VIP aims to explore a portable player identity where activity, achievements, badges and community reputation may eventually be presented as one coherent journey.

“The match may finish. The player’s legacy should keep growing.”

FOR PLAYERS

A profile that tells a fuller story.

Players may eventually present selected games, badges, challenge history and reputation signals through one Players League VIP identity.

DISTRIBUTION PRINCIPLES

Transparency before allocation.

A final public distribution structure has not yet been published. No percentages should be invented or implied before allocations, custody arrangements, vesting and intended uses are formally approved and disclosed.

✓

Publish exact allocations before any public distribution.

✓

Identify controlled wallets and their intended purpose.

✓

Disclose vesting or lockups where applicable.

✓

Separate operating funds from liquidity and community allocations.

✓

Update the White Paper when verified facts change.

09

GOVERNANCE DIRECTION

Community voice without pretending every decision is decentralised.

Players League VIP may experiment with polls, proposals and non-binding community signals. Until a formal governance system is implemented and documented, operational, security, legal and product decisions remain with the project team.

Community may help signal

Feature priorities, supported games, challenge ideas and community initiatives.

The team must retain responsibility

Security, compliance, treasury controls, emergency actions and contractual obligations.

10

SECURITY AND OPERATIONS

Security is an ongoing process.

01Verified contract information should always be linked to the official BaseScan record.

02Private keys and secret API keys must never be exposed in public code or browsers.

03Database access should use authentication, least privilege and Row Level Security.

04Liquidity and treasury actions should use documented controls before meaningful funds are introduced.

05Incidents and material changes should be communicated clearly to the community.
